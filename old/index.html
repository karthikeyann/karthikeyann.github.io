I am a Senior Software Engineer at NVIDIA, building GPU-accelerated data processing in CUDA: RAPIDS cuDF and the cuDF backend for the Velox query engine. From August 2014 to May 2019, I was a CAD DFT Engineer at NVIDIA, developing deep learning solutions for EDA problems, DFT algorithms, and applied parallel computing.
I did M.S (Research) at the Department of Electrical Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Madras (IITM) . I worked in GPGPU parallel computing, VLSI CAD algorithms, FPGA implementations, Signal processing and communication algorithms.

My research interests include GPU-accelerated Databases, Data Processing, Parallel Computing (CUDA & Multi‑core), Machine Learning, Deep Learning, HW accelerators and EDA Algorithms
